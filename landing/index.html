How it works

From launch to leaderboard in three steps

1

Launch

Organizers create a competition, set scoring criteria and submission rules, upload media, and assign judges.

2

Compete

Participants register solo or as a team, upload submissions, and the community votes and comments.

3

Award

Judges score against each criterion, winners are selected automatically, and everyone is notified.
